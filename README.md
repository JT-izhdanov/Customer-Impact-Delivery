# Customer Impact Delivery — 2027 Plan

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · **Owner:** Igor Zhdanov (Practice Director, Data & AI) · **Audience:** JourneyTeam leadership

## What this is

JourneyTeam's 2027 plan to shift how we sell and deliver: from practice-specific technology projects to engagements anchored on **Customer Impact** — measurable business outcomes that typically require capabilities from more than one practice.

The 2025 practice consolidation (down to four practices) changed the org chart but not the deal flow. This plan targets the things the merger didn't touch: what we sell, how we scope, how we staff, how we measure, and how we compensate.

## Core thesis

> Customers don't buy Fabric, Business Central, or Sentinel. They buy a faster close, fewer stockouts, lower risk, and more productive people. When we lead with the outcome, the multi-practice solution follows naturally — and the deal is bigger, stickier, and harder for a single-stack competitor to displace.
