# 08 — Metrics

What we measure to know whether the shift is working. Leading indicators monthly (PD council), lagging quarterly (leadership).

## Baseline first (Q4 2026)

Before setting targets, measure the current state:

- % of FY26 bookings involving 2+ practices (deal count and revenue).
- % of proposals >$100K that contained any quantified business outcome.
- Revenue per active account; # of practices selling into each of the top 30 accounts.
- Post-go-live touch rate (how many accounts have any structured cadence today).

## Leading indicators (monthly)

| Metric | Why it leads | 2027 signal of health |
|---|---|---|
| Business Outcome Discoveries sold | The front door is working | Growing quarter over quarter from pilot |
| % of qualified pipeline >threshold with an Impact Hypothesis | Sellers are running the motion | Trending to 100% |
| % of pipeline (>threshold) that is multi-practice | Solutioning is crossing practices | Above the FY26 baseline within 2 quarters |
| Second-practice reviews held vs. deals above threshold | The deal desk is functioning | ~100% |
| VRRs held vs. VRRs due | We're measuring impact, not just claiming it | ~100% |

## Lagging indicators (quarterly)

| Metric | Target (proposed, pending baseline) |
|---|---|
| % of new bookings multi-practice (revenue-weighted) | ≥25% by Q4 2027 |
| Average deal size, Customer Impact deals vs. traditional | Materially higher (expect 1.5–2×) |
| Net revenue retention: Impact accounts vs. siloed accounts | Impact accounts higher |
| Documented customer impact records (from VRRs) | ≥12 by year end |
| Referenceable case studies stated in business metrics | ≥5 by year end |
| Cross-sell opportunities sourced from VRRs | Tracked from Q2; becomes a pipeline source line |

## Anti-metrics (watch for gaming)

- **Fake multi-practice deals:** a token 5-hour workstream added to claim the accelerator. Rule: a workstream counts only above a floor (proposed: 10% of deal value or $15K).
- **Hypothesis theater:** Impact Hypotheses written to pass review, never revisited. Rule: VRR completion is the paired metric — a hypothesis without a later VRR scores zero.
- **Discovery stuffing:** discounted/free discoveries to hit the count. Rule: track discovery *revenue*, not just count.

## Kill criteria (honesty clause)

Declare the pilot unsuccessful and rework (not quietly abandon) if by end of Q2 2027:

- Fewer than 3 paid discoveries sold, or
- No pilot deal converted to a multi-practice Phase 1, or
- Sellers above threshold are bypassing the hypothesis requirement more than half the time.

Failure would most likely mean the incentive design (doc 07) didn't ship — fix that before blaming the framework.
