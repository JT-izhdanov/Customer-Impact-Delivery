# 04 — Cross-Practice Plays

Named, repeatable, sellable offerings. Each anchors on an outcome, involves 2+ practices by design, and has a small paid entry point that de-risks the customer's first step. Launch 2 in the Q1 pilot; the rest in Q2 (see doc 09).

**Format per play:** Outcome & buyer → practices → entry point → expand path → anchor metrics.

---

## Play 1 — AI-Ready, Secure Data Estate
*Proposed pilot play #1 (strong Data & AI + Security pairing; rides the market's #1 ask)*

- **Outcome / buyer:** "Deploy AI without leaking data or embarrassing ourselves." CIO/CISO.
- **Practices:** D&AI (lead) + SEC + C&D.
- **Entry point:** 3–4 week **AI Readiness & Risk Assessment** — data estate scan, Purview posture, oversharing exposure, Copilot readiness score, prioritized roadmap. Fixed fee.
- **Expand path:** Purview deployment & labeling (SEC) → data platform/Fabric foundation (D&AI) → Copilot rollout & adoption (C&D) → custom AI use cases (D&AI).
- **Anchor metrics:** % estate classified/labeled; oversharing findings closed; Copilot adoption %; first AI use case in production.

## Play 2 — Modern Finance: Faster Close, Sharper Forecast
*Proposed pilot play #2 (BC-led; pulls Data & AI into the ERP install base)*

- **Outcome / buyer:** "Close in 5 days, trust the forecast." CFO.
- **Practices:** BC (lead) + D&AI + C&D.
- **Entry point:** 2–3 week **Finance Impact Assessment** — close-process mapping, baseline days-to-close, reporting gaps, automation candidates.
- **Expand path:** BC optimization/implementation → Fabric/Power BI finance analytics & consolidation → Power Automate close tasks → Copilot for Finance.
- **Anchor metrics:** days to close; forecast variance; manual journal hours.

## Play 3 — Sales Velocity
- **Outcome / buyer:** "More pipeline converted, faster." CRO.
- **Practices:** C&D (lead) + D&AI.
- **Entry point:** **Revenue Process Assessment** — funnel baseline, CRM hygiene, forecast accuracy, rep time study.
- **Expand path:** D365 Sales optimization → Copilot for Sales → pipeline analytics & propensity models (Fabric/ML) → quote automation (with BC where ERP-connected).
- **Anchor metrics:** win rate; sales cycle days; forecast accuracy; quote turnaround.

## Play 4 — Secure Productivity (Copilot Impact)
- **Outcome / buyer:** "Copilot that provably pays for itself — safely." CIO + CISO jointly.
- **Practices:** C&D (lead) + SEC + D&AI.
- **Entry point:** **Copilot Value & Security Sprint** — pilot cohort, baseline task times, security guardrails, adoption plan.
- **Expand path:** tenant-wide secured rollout → Viva/usage analytics → Copilot Studio agents for line-of-business processes.
- **Anchor metrics:** hours saved per user; adoption/active-use %; security exceptions; agent use cases live.

## Play 5 — Order-to-Cash Acceleration
- **Outcome / buyer:** "Cash in the door faster, fewer order errors." COO/CFO.
- **Practices:** BC (lead) + C&D + D&AI.
- **Entry point:** **O2C Process Baseline** — cycle-time measurement across quote, order, fulfillment, invoice, collection.
- **Expand path:** BC + D365 process integration → Power Automate exception handling → O2C analytics and bottleneck detection (Fabric).
- **Anchor metrics:** order-to-cash days; DSO; order error rate.

## Play 6 — Risk & Compliance Confidence
- **Outcome / buyer:** "Pass the audit, qualify for insurance, sleep at night." CISO/CFO.
- **Practices:** SEC (lead) + D&AI + BC.
- **Entry point:** **Security & Compliance Baseline** — Secure Score, framework gap assessment (CMMC/HIPAA/SOC 2 as relevant), Purview posture.
- **Expand path:** Defender/Sentinel deployment → Purview information protection → compliance reporting automation (D&AI) → ERP controls (BC).
- **Anchor metrics:** Secure Score; framework gaps closed; audit prep hours; premium/coverage outcome.

## Design rules for all plays

1. **Small paid entry point (2–4 weeks, fixed fee).** Assessments produce the Value Baseline and Impact Hypothesis — the sales artifacts — while being genuinely useful standalone.
2. **The lead practice owns the play; the partner practices are named in the play definition,** not negotiated per deal. This removes the per-deal collaboration tax.
3. **Every play ends its first phase with a VRR date on the calendar.**
4. **Map each play to Microsoft funding** (assessments often qualify for workload-specific partner funding — ECIF/CSI-type programs). Outcome-led selling still cashes workload-based incentives; the mapping is an internal detail, not the customer pitch.
5. **One-pager per play** (to be built in `plays/` as they're finalized): pitch, qualifying questions, entry-point SOW template, Impact Hypothesis template.
