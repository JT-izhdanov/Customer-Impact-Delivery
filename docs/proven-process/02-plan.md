## Purpose

Plan prepares the project for execution by translating strategy into a clear, approved delivery plan. It begins **after a signed EOW** and continues until milestones, resources, and expectations are aligned and ready for delivery.

## How success is measured

**Primary: Project Milestones** *(metric still being finalized)* — a milestone is a significant event or outcome within a project, **recognized by the customer and JourneyTeam**, marking progress toward achieving the Flag Plant. Tracked in Project Operations at the task level.

**Leading indicators:**
- **Time from EOW signature to Target Start Date** — target: 4–6 weeks; kickoff should occur during the sprint matching the Target Start Date.
- **Initial sprints planned** — target: 3 sprints consistently planned ([sprint planning guide](https://journeyteam.sharepoint.com/sites/ProjectDelivery/SitePages/Project-Sprint-Planning.aspx); [internal planning report](https://jtp.crm.dynamics.com/crmreports/viewer/viewer.aspx?action=run&id=6038d40a-630c-f111-8406-000d3a3529aa&helpID=Planned%20Work.rdl&appid=78854deb-627f-e911-a818-000d3a3b5b14)).
- **Functional requirements** — target: artifacts completed within ~20% of overall project timeline.

## Exit gate — Plan stage gate formally approved by PM, PL, and customer POC

Before moving to Deliver:
1. All planning artifacts, approvals, and baselines in place:
   - Business requirements defined and validated by the customer
   - Project plan baselined (change orders approved as necessary; deliverables for each exit gate defined and validated; assignments/resources defined; 3 sprints planned)
2. Architecture and Technical Documents completed (if applicable)
3. **Flag Plant and Customer Impact confirmed.**

## Customer Impact integration points

**The anchor is already in the gate.** Exit criterion 3 — "Flag Plant and Customer Impact confirmed" — means the Proven Process already names Customer Impact as a Plan-stage requirement. The 2027 plan's job is to give that phrase operational teeth:

- **"Customer Impact confirmed" gets a definition.** Proposed: it means the **Impact Hypothesis is re-validated post-sale** — metric, baseline, target, and measurement plan confirmed with the customer's metric-owning executive, and the **Value Baseline actually captured** (not promised) before the plan is baselined. If Envision produced no hypothesis (below-threshold deals), the criterion stays as it works today.
- **Kickoff confirms outcome stewardship within the existing roles.** The kickoff already aligns roles; for Customer Impact engagements it confirms the PM's custody of the hypothesis and the PL's telemetry duties (doc 11 — no new role), and puts the **first VRR date on the calendar** — the cheapest moment to schedule it.
- **Discovery is where baseline data gets pulled.** Plan-stage Discovery validates business requirements; for impact engagements it also validates the *numbers* — pulling the baseline from source systems. If the customer can't produce them, the change-order mechanism (already in the gate) scopes the baseline workstream (doc 02).
- **Requirements trace to the metric.** The functional-requirements artifact gains one column: which outcome/metric each major requirement serves. This is the mid-delivery steering mechanism ("are we still building the thing that moves the metric?" — doc 06) and it costs a column, not a process.
- **Milestones metric dovetails perfectly.** The in-progress milestone definition — "recognized by the customer... marking progress toward achieving the Flag Plant" — is precisely the plan's language of customer-acknowledged value gates. As this metric is finalized (PMO), it's the natural place to distinguish *deliverable* milestones from *impact* milestones (doc 06 lifecycle). Worth a working session with PMO leadership while the definition is still wet.

### Tension to resolve (routed to backlog)

The 4–6 week EOW-to-start target and "3 sprints planned" measure **delivery readiness speed**. Baseline capture adds work at exactly that point. If baselining isn't scoped as a *deliverable in the EOW* (see `envision/06-eow-sent.md`), it will be squeezed out by the very leading indicators Plan is measured on. The fix is upstream (EOW line item), but the failure would appear here — PMO and Delivery leaders should own the watch.
