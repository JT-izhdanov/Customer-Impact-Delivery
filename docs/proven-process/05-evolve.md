## Customer Impact integration points

Evolve is the stage the entire 2027 plan is built to power. Its title is the plan's thesis ("Becoming a Trusted Advisor" — the board's Seat 1 language), its stated purpose includes "validating impact," and its exit gate (a new project lead) is the cross-sell moment the diagnosis said we were missing (cause 7). What's missing is only the instrumentation:

- **The Validate sub-stage is the VRR's home.** The stage description says Evolve drives value "through validating impact" — the Value Realization Review (doc 06) is exactly that, made concrete: metric vs. baseline vs. target, adoption data, blockers, next opportunity, on a quarterly cadence, with the metric-owning executive. The Launch gate's open question ("is the Flag Plant on track to be realized?") gets its dated, numbered answer here.
- **The 70 QBRs/quarter are the delivery vehicle — upgrade, don't add.** A team already committed to ~70 quarterly business reviews doesn't need a new meeting; it needs the **Impact Scorecard** (Few's one-pager: baseline → target → actual, adoption trend, dollars attributed, next opportunity) as the QBR's first page for impact accounts. Same calendar, transformed content: from "how's the system?" to "here's what it's earning you."
- **"Opportunities Created with Existing Customer" is the plan's cross-sell metric — give it provenance.** Track which opportunities originate from a VRR/QBR's "next-highest-impact" agenda item. That's the attribution hook for rewarding outcome tending (doc 07: VRR-sourced opportunities credit the practice of whoever surfaced them — account team, PM, or PL) and the evidence that the machinery works.
- **Retention math meets Gardner's curve.** Evolve's headline metric (12-month billed retention) is exactly where the research predicts Customer Impact pays: multi-practice clients retain better and survive personnel departures (research 02, ~18× revenue at 5 practices). Doc 08's "NRR: impact accounts vs. siloed accounts" comparison can be computed directly from this existing report — no new instrumentation.
- **The exit gate makes the flywheel explicit.** "New Non-Support Project Lead" → Envision. For impact accounts, that lead should arrive *outcome-labeled* (born from a VRR finding, with baseline evidence attached) — the highest-quality lead type the firm can generate, and the antidote to diagnosis cause 1 (tech-labeled leads).
- **Support cases are impact sensors.** Case patterns (recurring user errors, workaround tickets) are early warnings that adoption — and therefore the metric — is drifting. Support Case Success/Resolution already gets measured; routing case-pattern summaries into the QBR/VRR prep closes the loop for free.

### Structural note (for the plan docs and the board)

The Proven Process's own arc — Envision's business-outcome language → Plan's "Customer Impact confirmed" gate → Deliver's flag-plant alignment → Launch's "outcomes realized?" question → **Evolve's "validate impact"** — means the 2027 plan is not a new process. It is the **instrumentation of promises the Proven Process already makes.** This framing should lead the executive summary: nothing about the Proven Process changes; what changes is that its outcome language finally gets numbers, owners, and dates.
