# Deliver 3 of 3 — Execute

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

To Execute means to bring the plan to life as teams build, configure, and deliver against sprint commitments. Progress is actively managed to ensure work stays aligned to scope, timelines, and the flag plant.

## Key actions

- Monitor, track & manage Commitments through sprint progress

## What "good" looks like

- Sprint commitments are met
- Work stays on track with milestones
- Risks and blockers are addressed quickly
- Deliverables meet success criteria
- Progress aligns to the flag plant

## Customer Impact integration points

Execute is deliberately lean — monitoring and managing commitments — so the impact additions are lean too:

- **Measurement commitments are tracked like build commitments.** Baseline refreshes, telemetry setup, and adoption instrumentation entered the sprint as commitments (deliver/01); Execute's existing monitor-track-manage discipline covers them with no special handling. A slipped telemetry task is a slipped commitment, visible in Sprint Commitment Met.
- **Impact blockers use the existing escalation path.** Two blockers specific to impact engagements: the customer's 50% not arriving (data access, metric-owner availability — contracted at the Solution Meeting), and adoption prerequisites slipping. Both are "risks and blockers addressed quickly" — routed to the Outcome Owner via the decision rights defined in Plan-stage Document.
- **"Progress aligns to the flag plant" inherits the metric.** For impact engagements the flag plant carries the Impact Hypothesis (per the Envision integration); Execute's alignment check therefore *is* the outcome alignment check — no parallel tracking needed.
