# Deliver 2 of 3 — Customer Review

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

The Review sub-stage brings the plan to life as teams build, configure, and deliver against sprint commitments. Progress is actively managed to ensure work stays aligned to scope, timelines, and the flag plant.

## Key actions

- Before client review, ensure solution testing to success criteria
  - At end of Sprint, review completion of committed deliverables with customers including a functional demo, show & tell to ensure alignment with Flag Plant
- Show and tell
- Retro

## What "good" looks like

- Work is clearly demonstrated and understood
- Deliverables meet expectations
- Feedback is captured and addressed
- Risks and concerns are proactively discussed
- Next steps and decisions are clear

## Customer Impact integration points

The sprint review is the recurring customer touchpoint — the natural carrier for the impact thread at near-zero added cost:

- **Demos narrated in outcome language.** "Show & tell to ensure alignment with Flag Plant" already exists; for impact engagements the demo opens with the metric it serves: *"you said 12 days to close costs you $2M — this is the piece that removes 3 of those days."* Same demo, different first sentence — and it keeps the customer's executive sponsor attending, which protects CER.
- **The mid-phase impact check lives in the retro.** Once every few sprints, the retro adds one question: *is the baseline still valid, and are we still building what moves the metric?* (doc 06). Five minutes; catches scope drift the budget metrics can't see.
- **Feedback is triaged against the metric.** Customer feedback naturally accretes scope. The story-to-metric tags give reviews a principled response: feedback that moves the metric competes for capacity; feedback that doesn't goes to the post-go-live backlog (which the Deliver exit gate already requires). This protects both margin and outcome.
- **Sprint reviews rehearse the VRR.** A customer accustomed to seeing progress framed against their metric every sprint will find the first Value Realization Review (doc 06) familiar rather than novel — the review cadence quietly builds the measurement relationship the whole model depends on.
