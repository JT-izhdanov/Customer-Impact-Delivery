# Launch 3 of 3 — Stabilize

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

The Stabilize sub-stage brings focus to supporting users and resolving issues following go-live. Teams provide hypercare, monitor performance, and ensure a smooth transition to ongoing operations.

## Key actions

- **Post Go-Live stabilization**
  - **Hypercare/Post Go-Live Support:** an intensive, short-term support period immediately following Go-Live. Focus: stabilize the environment, resolve issues quickly, and support end users as they adapt to the new solution. Typical duration: 1–7 days, or as outlined in EOW.
  - **Operational State:** following hypercare, the solution undergoes a formal operational transition, at which point ownership is transferred to operations and the project is closed. Typical duration: depends on project requirements.

## What "good" looks like

- Users are supported during hypercare
- Issues are resolved quickly
- The system is stable and reliable
- **Adoption is progressing**
- Transition to operations is complete

## Customer Impact integration points

"Adoption is progressing" is already on Stabilize's good-list — the plan makes it observable and gives it a landing place:

- **Adoption gets a number during hypercare.** The telemetry validated at Deploy produces its first real readings here. "Progressing" means tracking against the adoption success criteria set at the Deliver exit gate — visible on the same dashboard the VRR will use. Hypercare fixes *system* issues; the adoption readout catches *usage* issues (a stable system nobody uses is a stabilization failure the ticket queue won't show).
- **Operational transition hands off the measurement, not just the system.** The formal transition to operations includes: who refreshes the metric data, who watches adoption telemetry, and the confirmed first-VRR date (set at kickoff, now weeks away). This is the moment the PM hands the outcome thread to the account team (SAM/AE + Customer Advocacy) — through existing closure duties, before the project team disengages (doc 11).
- **Hypercare's end is not the impact's end — say so to the customer.** The 1–7 day hypercare window closes long before the metric moves. The closing communication should explicitly separate the two: "support transitions today; your outcome review is on [date]." This single sentence prevents the customer reading project close as JourneyTeam's exit from the result — and tees up Evolve.
