# Evolve 2 of 3 — Manage

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

The Manage sub-stage brings JourneyTeam and the customer together through ongoing engagement, ensuring strong relationships, proactive communication, and alignment on evolving business needs.

## Key items

### Customer Advocacy
- **Completion of Project Retrospective**
  - Preferred option: live customer retrospective discussion
    - If unable to secure a discussion with the customer: an internal assessment of the project (insights from Project Team, Sales, and Finance) is reviewed by the Internal Validation Committee as to whether the project is a positive or negative add to the Customer Referencability calculation
  - Further outputs of the live retrospective: whether the customer is added to the **Testimonial or Case Study pipeline** (managed by Customer Advocacy) and whether the customer joins the **Customer Reference Program**

### Trusted Advisor
- **Step 1: Set up the expectation for Trusted Advisor**
  - Establish with the customer what a trusted advisory relationship is vs. a vendor relationship
  - Establish what **we** will do:
    - Conduct Quarterly Business Reviews
    - Provide valuable information & insights relevant to the customer and their industry
    - Communicate weekly regarding relevant updates and info that helps the customer move their business forward
  - Establish what **the customer** needs to do:
    - Agree to Strategic/Quarterly Business Reviews
    - Agree to weekly communication
    - **Provide business KPIs and details on strategic initiatives**
- **Step 2: Agreement to be strategic**
- **Weekly Connections**
- **SBR (Strategic Business Review)**
  - First 15 minutes: validate adoption and usage of current systems
    - Identify any gaps and address them
    - Identify any new project phases or functionality beneficial to the client
  - 45+ minutes: discuss current business challenges and strategic initiatives
  - [Breakdown of SBR Process and Scripts](https://journeyteam.sharepoint.com/sites/sales/_layouts/15/guestaccess.aspx?share=IgD0TPlfCs6KQ5hy73q0aSqFARpjzFPb7SxeF2U_silZ08E&e=Rfudn7)

## What "good" looks like

- Regular communication and engagement with the customer is maintained
- Trusted advisor relationship is established and strengthened
- Customer needs, priorities, and challenges are continuously understood
- Strategic conversations (e.g., QBRs) are conducted consistently
- Risks, concerns, and opportunities are proactively addressed
- Alignment is maintained on current and future direction

## Customer Impact integration points

Manage already runs a formal Trusted Advisor program with mutual obligations — the plan's job is to arm it with evidence:

- **The SBR's first 15 minutes are the Impact Scorecard's slot.** The SBR already opens by validating adoption and usage; for impact accounts that quarter-hour presents the scorecard: adoption trend *plus* metric vs. baseline vs. target. Then the 45+ strategic minutes run on evidence instead of anecdote — "the last initiative moved DSO 6 days; here are the next three candidates ranked by the same math."
- **Trusted Advisor Step 1 already contracts the customer's 50%.** "Provide business KPIs and details on strategic initiatives" is *literally* Block's customer-side obligation for measurability (session 01). The plan adds precision: for impact accounts, the KPI commitment names the metrics, their data owner, and refresh cadence — the measurement plan and the Trusted Advisor agreement become one conversation, not two asks.
- **Maister's warning applies to Step 1's framing.** Establishing "advisor vs. vendor" expectations works only if the weekly communications and QBRs deliver genuinely client-first insight (low self-orientation). The insight content built for Envision (per-play benchmarks, industry teaching narratives — cause 3) does double duty here as the "valuable information & insights" the program promises.
- **The Case Study pipeline is where quantified impact records land.** The live retrospective already routes customers to Testimonial/Case Study/Reference programs; impact records from Validate give that pipeline what research 04 says nobody in the mid-market publishes — client-verified business numbers. Proposed: case-study candidacy is assessed *after* the first VRR, when there's a result to tell.
- **The Internal Validation Committee gains one input.** When judging whether a project adds positively to referencability, the committee should see the impact record (metric moved or not) alongside Project/Sales/Finance views — protecting the reference program from "delivered fine, changed nothing" entries.
- **Weekly Connections carry adoption pulses.** The committed weekly touch is the natural channel for lightweight telemetry signals between QBRs ("Copilot active use dipped 12% after the org change — worth a nudge?") — small proof, every week, that we watch their outcomes.
