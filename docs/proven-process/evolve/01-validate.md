## Customer Impact integration points

Validate's good-list *is* the Value Realization Review agenda, item for item — measured outcomes, adoption validation, next opportunities (doc 06). This sub-stage is where the 2027 plan stops being a proposal and becomes an existing process step with new instruments:

- **"Outcomes and impact are measured" gets its method.** Today the sub-stage names the *what*; the plan supplies the *how*: metric vs. the baseline captured in Plan-stage Discovery, relative to the go-live epoch (T₀, launch/02), on the quarterly VRR cadence, presented as the one-page Impact Scorecard (Few: baseline → target → actual, adoption trend, dollars attributed, next opportunity).
- **The first Validate session has a date before delivery starts.** Set at Project Kickoff, carried through the Launch handoff — 60–90 days post-go-live (research 03). Without the pre-set date, Validate happens "when we get to it," which is the old cause-7 failure wearing a new name.
- **"Next opportunities are identified" gains provenance tracking.** Opportunities born here get tagged VRR-sourced — feeding Evolve's "Opportunities Created with Existing Customer" indicator, the doc 07 origination credit, and the proof that outcome tending generates pipeline.
- **Each Validate session emits an impact record.** One page, archived per account: the raw material for case studies (Customer Advocacy pipeline, evolve/02), reference asks (Launch's Referenceable Customer metric), and the plan's own evidence base (doc 08's "documented customer impact records").
