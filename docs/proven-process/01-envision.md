# Proven Process — Stage 1: Envision

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026. Internal links point to JourneyTeam SharePoint/Dynamics — access-controlled.*

## Purpose

Envision lays the foundation for success by aligning strategy, value, and partnership **before delivery begins**. It starts with the first interaction with a potential customer and continues until a new project is confirmed through a **signed EOW**.

- **Primary roles:** Marketing, Sales, Presales.
- **Method:** the JourneyTeam Envision Sales Process, which focuses on driving **Business Outcomes** using **Status and Scenario questions**.

## Customer Impact integration points (how the 2027 plan lands inside Envision)

The good news: Envision already claims outcome DNA — "driving Business Outcomes by using Status and Scenario questions." Customer Impact doesn't replace the stage; it deepens what happens inside its existing sub-stages:

| Envision sub-stage | Customer Impact addition |
|---|---|
| Go-To-Market / Lead Outreach | Plays (doc 04) become the campaign/packaging layer; the Outcome Catalog (doc 03) supplies the outcome language per buyer persona |
| Strategic Alignment | The "move off the solution" reframe: tech-labeled lead → business outcome conversation; qualify the metric-owning executive |
| Strategy Session | Runs as (or sells) the **Business Outcome Discovery** (doc 05); Status/Scenario questions extended with Evidence → Impact quantification |
| Solution Meeting | Cross-practice solution review happens *before* this meeting (doc 05 deal rules); solution presented against the Impact Hypothesis |
| EOW Sent | Impact Hypothesis attached to every EOW above threshold; measurement plan and first VRR date written into the EOW |

**Exit-gate addition proposed by the plan (decision for leadership):** deals above the threshold add one item to the existing three — *Impact Hypothesis attached and signed by the customer's metric-owning executive.* Below threshold, nothing changes.

### Tension to resolve (routed to backlog)

Envision's success metrics are all **volume metrics** (SQLs, pipeline $, close %, booked revenue). The Customer Impact plan adds **quality/mix metrics** (% of pipeline multi-practice, % of proposals with a quantified Impact Hypothesis, discovery revenue — doc 08). These are complementary, not conflicting — but if leadership inspects only the volume metrics, the outcome motion will lose every prioritization fight (exactly the session-01 diagnosis, cause 0). Where the mix metrics get inspected, and by whom, is an open design question for the Envision leaders named above.
