# Plan 2 of 3 — Discovery

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

Discovery brings JourneyTeam and the customer together to understand current and future state processes, define requirements, and identify key needs. Teams collaborate to gather insights, shape user stories, and ensure alignment on what will be delivered.

## Key actions

- Per each stakeholder group (change management)
- Current and future state process mapping
- User story creation

## What "good" looks like

- Processes and requirements are clearly defined
- User needs and priorities are validated
- Risks and gaps are identified early
- Scope is refined and aligned
- Teams are aligned on what will be delivered

## Customer Impact integration points

Discovery's existing actions are one small extension away from being the plan's measurement machinery:

- **Process mapping gains numbers.** Current-state maps already get drawn; for impact engagements each mapped process carries its **cycle time / cost / error rate** — which *is* the Value Baseline (doc 02), captured as a by-product of work already happening. Future-state maps carry the target values. No new workshop; one more row on the map.
- **User stories trace to the metric.** Story format extends from "as a [user] I want [capability]" to include *"...which contributes to [outcome metric]"* for the stories that carry the impact. This creates the requirements-to-metric traceability the Plan exit gate's "Customer Impact confirmed" needs, and makes mid-delivery steering possible ("are we still building what moves the metric?" — doc 06).
- **Stakeholder-group work is the adoption map.** Discovery already works per stakeholder group for change management; that same structure identifies **whose behavior must change for the metric to move** — the input the Training Strategy (kickoff) and adoption telemetry need.
- **"Risks and gaps identified early" includes measurement gaps.** If baseline data can't be produced from the customer's systems, that's a Discovery finding — triggering the change-order path (Plan exit gate) to scope a baseline workstream, typically Data & AI-led (doc 02). Finding it here costs a change order; finding it at the VRR costs the model's credibility.
