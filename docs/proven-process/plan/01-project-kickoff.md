# Plan 1 of 3 — Project Kickoff

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

The Project Kickoff brings JourneyTeam and the customer together to align on goals, confirm the flag plant, and establish the foundation for delivery. Teams align on roles, communication, timelines, and next steps to ensure the project starts with clarity and shared expectations.

## Key actions

- **Project Intake**
  - Project repository set up in JTP with corresponding Teams/SharePoint
- Project teams assembled by JT and customer
- Internal Sales to Delivery Kickoff completed
- Pre-kickoff alignment with customer PM + JT PM
- Kickoff Meetings for:
  - Internal project team (as needed)
  - JT and Customer teams (as needed)
- Flag Plant reviewed and confirmed with the customer; teams, governance, goals, roadmap, communication cadence confirmed
- Support Strategy discussed
- Training Strategy defined
- Meeting Cadence established and scheduled

## What "good" looks like

- Goals, scope, and success criteria are aligned
- Flag plant is confirmed
- Roles and responsibilities are clear
- Communication cadence is established
- Team is aligned and ready to begin

## Customer Impact integration points

Kickoff already aligns roles, goals, and cadence — the plan threads impact into each existing action rather than adding meetings:

- **Sales to Delivery Kickoff is the hypothesis handoff.** The single highest-risk moment for outcome continuity: the Impact Hypothesis (signed in Envision) transfers here with its context — metric, baseline, customer's 50%, yellow lights. If it only lives in the seller's head, the outcome dies at this handoff. Proposed: the internal kickoff agenda gains one standing item — *walk the delivery team through the Impact Hypothesis*.
- **Team assembly = pod assembly, same three roles.** For multi-practice engagements, "project teams assembled" follows the play's named practice mix (doc 04, rule 2) — still exactly one PM, one PL, and Project Developers (drawn from the contributing practices). Outcome-stewardship duties (PM: hypothesis custody; PL: metric traceability/telemetry — doc 11) are confirmed among the roles presented to the customer.
- **Flag Plant review is the impact re-confirmation.** The existing "Flag Plant reviewed and confirmed" action is where "Customer Impact confirmed" (Plan exit gate, criterion 3) begins: re-validate the metric and target with the customer's metric-owning executive present — not just the customer PM.
- **Meeting cadence includes the VRR.** While the cadence calendar is open, the first Value Realization Review (60–90 days post-go-live) goes on it. A date set at kickoff survives; one "scheduled later" doesn't.
- **Training Strategy doubles as the adoption plan's skeleton.** Adoption is what converts deliverables into impact (and MAU into Microsoft incentives — research 01); the existing Training Strategy action is its natural home, extended with usage-telemetry expectations.
