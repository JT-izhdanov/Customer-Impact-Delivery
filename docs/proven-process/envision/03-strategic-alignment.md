## Customer Impact integration points

Strategic Alignment is the Proven Process home of the outcome reframe — the plan adds rigor to actions that already exist here:

- **"Define Business Objectives and Outcomes" is the hook.** Customer Impact upgrades it from a statement to a *quantified* artifact: objective → metric → current baseline → target → value estimate (the Impact Hypothesis skeleton, doc 02). The Evidence → Impact questioning method (Khalsa: "How much? How do you know? What does it cost you?") is the how.
- **BANT's "A" gets sharpened:** Authority for Customer Impact deals means *the executive who owns the metric*, not just a budget holder (doc 05 exec-sponsor rule). Proposed: capture "metric owner identified: Y/N" at opportunity creation — it's the single best predictor of whether the outcome motion applies.
- **"Identify issues that could impede objectives"** maps to Khalsa's yellow lights — voiced early, logged on the opportunity, honored (no hoping they resolve).
- **Envision Sales Agent:** the highest-leverage enablement channel found so far. The agent can carry the plan's insight content per play (teaching narratives, outcome questions per persona, benchmark data) and coach the "move off the solution" reframe in the flow of work — no classroom training dependency. This directly addresses diagnosis cause 3 (sellers *can't* challenge the label) using a tool that already exists.
- **Multi-practice check happens here, informally:** when the stated objective spans practices (most do — doc 03), the seller flags it at qualification so the cross-practice solution review (doc 05) is scheduled before the Solution Meeting, not after scoping is done.
