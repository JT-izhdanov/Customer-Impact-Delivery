# Envision 1 of 6 — Go-To-Market

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

Drives pipeline and revenue by positioning JourneyTeam's value in the market, building awareness, and creating opportunities to engage the right customers.

## Activities

- **Define Targeting & Messaging** — Partner with Sales and Practice Leads to align on target accounts, messaging frameworks, and offer positioning.
- **Execute Demand Generation** — Design and run multi-channel campaigns that nurture prospects from initial awareness through qualification and handoff to Sales.
- **Optimize Performance** — Use data and insights to continuously refine targeting, messaging, and channel effectiveness.
- **Measure & Improve** — Track key metrics (open rates, reply rates, conversions, influenced pipeline) and provide clear, actionable recommendations.
- **Enable Sales Execution** — Develop and maintain content and assets that support both brand awareness and sales engagement.
- **Activate Customer Advocacy** — Identify and engage satisfied customers to build references, case studies, and success stories that strengthen credibility and accelerate sales.
- **Sales Operations Support** — Enables efficient lead flow and revenue execution:
  - Support Sales participation in Microsoft GTM programs, including Partner Center lead sharing and funded engagement processes
  - Ensure CPOR is properly claimed to align JourneyTeam with customer relationships and won deals
  - Support CSP licensing processes, including quoting for new and renewal opportunities

## Customer Impact integration points

Go-To-Market is where the shift is *cheapest* to start — no comp or process change required, just what we say to the market:

- **Targeting & Messaging:** the "Define Targeting & Messaging" activity is the natural home for the where-to-play choices the board flagged (session 01) — target accounts and messaging chosen per **play** (doc 04) and buyer outcome (doc 03), not per technology.
- **Demand Generation:** campaigns framed on outcomes ("close in 5 days") generate leads that arrive *already outcome-labeled* — directly attacking diagnosis cause 1 (tech-labeled leads) at the source, before any seller has to reframe anything.
- **Customer Advocacy:** this existing activity is the delivery vehicle for the plan's biggest identified whitespace (research 04): case studies with **client-verified business metrics**, fed by VRR impact records (doc 06). The advocacy machinery exists; the plan gives it quantified raw material.
- **Sales Operations Support:** already owns CPOR and funded-engagement processes — the same machinery the plan's funding-stack approach depends on (research 01: ECIF, MCI engagements, per-workload PoE). Outcome SOWs modularized for claimability (doc 04, rule 4) land here operationally.
- **Enable Sales Execution:** the commercial-insight content the board says sellers lack (session 01, cause 3 — teaching narratives, benchmarks, cost-of-status-quo data) is produced under this activity.
