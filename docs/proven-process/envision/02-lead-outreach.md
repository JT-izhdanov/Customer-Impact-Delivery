# Envision 2 of 6 — Lead Outreach

*Source: internal Proven Process content provided by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026.*

Lead Outreach focuses on timely engagement with inbound and outbound leads. Sales teams follow structured outreach sequences, qualify prospects, and guide early conversations to determine whether an opportunity is worth pursuing.

## Key actions

- Within **24 hours** of an outreach lead being assigned to Sales, add the lead to the appropriate lead sequence (based on topic of interest); follow the sequence until the lead is Qualified or Disqualified.
- Add prospecting contacts to Contact sequences and create a lead upon engagement; progress the lead to Qualified or Disqualified following lead development activities.
- Leverage JourneyTeam tools, Microsoft GTM programs, and personal networks to generate, manage, and advance leads.
- Leverage our reporting to help leadership coach on the correct areas to provide additional focus.

## What "good" looks like

- Outreach is targeted and relevant
- Conversations focus on business outcomes
- Leads are engaged and qualified
- Next steps are clearly defined
- Handoffs are smooth and documented

## Customer Impact integration points

- **Sequences are keyed on "topic of interest" — today that's usually a technology.** The plan's lightest-touch change: build sequences keyed on **outcomes/plays** (doc 04) alongside tech topics, so an inbound "Power BI" lead can be worked with a "forecast accuracy" sequence. The 24-hour SLA and sequence discipline stay exactly as they are.
- **"Conversations focus on business outcomes" is already the stated bar.** Customer Impact supplies what's been missing to hit it: the qualifying questions per buyer persona (doc 03) and the commercial-insight openers (session 01, cause 3) sellers can actually use in a first conversation. The standard doesn't change; the ammunition to meet it does.
- **Qualification gains one question:** *does this lead connect to an executive who owns a business metric?* Not a gate at this stage — just captured on the lead, so downstream sub-stages know whether the outcome motion applies (deal-threshold rule, doc 05).
- **Reporting/coaching hook:** the existing coaching-via-reporting practice is where the plan's leading indicators (doc 08 — % of qualified leads with an outcome captured) get inspected without inventing a new forum.
