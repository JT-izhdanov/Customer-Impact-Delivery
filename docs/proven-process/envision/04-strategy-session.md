## Customer Impact integration points

The Strategy Session is where the plan's **Business Outcome Discovery** (doc 05) either happens or gets sold:

- **Two modes, one sub-stage.** For smaller deals, the Strategy Session *is* the discovery — outcomes validated conversationally, hypothesis sketched. For deals above the threshold, the Strategy Session's output is a **paid discovery/assessment engagement** (the play entry points, doc 04): the session frames the outcome and sells the 2–4 week baseline work. This keeps paid discovery inside the Proven Process instead of bolting on a new stage.
- **"Measurable Value" already appears in the language** — the plan adds the mechanics: leave the session with metric, baseline owner, and value estimate drafted, or with a scoped baseline workstream when the customer can't produce the number (a Data & AI entry point, doc 02).
- **Flag Plant ↔ Impact Hypothesis:** the Flag Plant sets the foundation for success; the Impact Hypothesis makes its success measurable. Proposal: for above-threshold deals, the Flag Plant references the hypothesis's outcome statement so both artifacts point at the same result. (How Flag Plant and Impact Hypothesis relate structurally is a design question for the Envision leaders — flagged in backlog.)
- **Showcases become outcome showcases:** demos framed against the customer's stated metric ("here's how this closes your 12-day gap"), and — as VRR impact records accumulate (doc 06) — showcases of *measured customer results*, the differentiator research 04 says nobody in the mid-market has.
- **Cross-practice solutioning is locked here:** the second-practice review (doc 05) must happen before or during Strategy Session, while the approach is still fluid — after this point the deal shape is set (session 01 crux).
