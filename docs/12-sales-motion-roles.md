# 12 — Roles in the Customer Impact Sales Motion

Who does what in the outcome-led Envision motion. Grounded in three firm facts: **we sell to SMB/SMC, we generate our own leads, and sellers carry practice-neutral quotas.** And one constraint: this fleshes out *sales-side* roles — the project roles (PM/PL/Developers) are constants (doc 11) and start at Plan.

## Design principles

1. **Hats, not hires.** SMB/SMC deal economics can't carry heavyweight enterprise sales roles (dedicated value engineers, industry principals). Every function below maps to an existing seat; where a new capability is needed, it's a trained hat on an existing role plus content/tooling support.
2. **Practice-neutral sellers are the asset — arm them.** A seller with no practice quota is already free to sell any outcome. What they lack is ammunition (insight content) and backup (solution consulting across four practices). The roles design is about supply lines to the seller, not new selling roles.
3. **The Envision Sales Agent is a role.** In an SMB/SMC motion, the AI agent is how one seller covers four practices' worth of outcome knowledge without four specialists in every deal.

## The roles

### 1. Marketing / Demand Generation (existing — CMO org)
The motion starts here because the leads are ours.
- **Today:** targeting, campaigns, content, advocacy, sales ops support (Go-To-Market sub-stage).
- **Customer Impact duties:** build campaigns and sequences **keyed to plays and outcomes**, not products; produce the per-play **insight kit** with the practices (teaching narrative, 2–3 benchmarks, cost-of-status-quo math, qualifying questions); publish quantified impact stories from VRR records (Customer Advocacy pipeline).
- **Why it's the highest-leverage role:** cause 1 (tech-labeled leads) is self-inflicted through this function — and therefore fixable by this function alone, with no dependency on seller behavior change.

### 2. Sales Development / Lead Outreach (existing sellers running sequences; *confirm whether dedicated SDRs exist*)
- **Today:** 24-hour SLA, topic-of-interest sequences, qualify/disqualify (Lead Outreach sub-stage).
- **Customer Impact duties:** run **outcome-keyed sequences** alongside tech-keyed ones; capture two fields at qualification — *stated business outcome* and *metric-owning executive identified (Y/N)*. That's the whole change at this stage.

### 3. Account Executive (AE — new-logo seller, existing)
Owns the deal end to end; the role the motion most depends on.
- **Today:** Strategic Alignment → Strategy Session → Solution Meeting → EOW; BANT; flag plant.
- **Customer Impact duties:** run the **outcome reframe** ("move off the solution") using the play's insight kit; qualify to the **metric-owning executive** (the sharpened "A" in BANT); own the **Impact Hypothesis** — the AE is its author and its advocate through signature; sell the paid discovery when the deal warrants it; disqualify honestly when there's no metric owner (price it as a tech project instead).
- **Profile note (Challenger research, board seat 5):** staff the pilot with the sellers who already teach and take control — and coach the rest with the first ten hypotheses written *with* them.

### 4. Strategic Account Manager (SAM — existing-account seller, existing)
The flywheel role: in our SMB/SMC base, expansion is cheaper than acquisition.
- **Today:** account ownership, retros for smaller projects, receiving leads from support's 20-hour rule and project closure handoffs.
- **Customer Impact duties:** account planning on **outcome whitespace** (which Outcome Catalog rows are open per account); carry the outcome thread post-close (from the PM's closure handoff); run VRR/SBR first-15-minutes with the Impact Scorecard; convert "next-highest-impact opportunity" into outcome-labeled opportunities (provenance-tagged, doc 07).

### 5. Solution Consultant / Presales (existing — VP of Client Solutions org)
The technical conscience of the motion — and the **value-quantification hat** lives here.
- **Today:** solution design, showcases, estimates for the EOW.
- **Customer Impact duties:** design across the play's practice mix (with Practice Director input); run the **baseline math** in discovery — turn the customer's "orders ship late" into "4.2 days average, costing ~$2M/yr" (the Evidence→Impact skill, trained from the Khalsa method + vendor value-engineering patterns, research 03); own the **measurement plan** draft that ships with the EOW; build the consumption/licensing forecast where funding applies.
- **This is the plan's biggest capability build:** value quantification is a trainable skill with templates (GitLab's public handbook is the free starting point), not a new hire. One "Value Lead" hat per practice-pair is enough for the pilot.

### 6. Practice Director (existing)
- **Today:** pre-approval in the Envision exit gate; bench and P&L ownership.
- **Customer Impact duties:** own their practice's **plays** and the insight-kit content (the practice knows the benchmarks; marketing packages them); staff the **second-practice review** (30 minutes, deals above threshold — the deal-desk function, doc 07); pre-approve with an outcome lens (is the hypothesis credible, is the practice mix right).

### 7. Sales Operations (existing — under Go-To-Market)
- **Today:** Partner Center programs, CPOR, CSP quoting.
- **Customer Impact duties:** **provenance tags** on leads and opportunities (outcome-labeled? VRR-sourced? play campaign?) so doc 08's mix metrics are queryable, not anecdotal; **modular EOW structure** so per-workload funding claims (MCI/CSP/FastTrack) survive outcome-shaped deals; maintain the play-to-funding mapping.

### 8. Envision Sales Agent (existing AI agent)
- **Today:** coaching and suggested next steps in Strategic Alignment and Strategy Session.
- **Customer Impact duties:** carry the insight kits (per-play teaching narratives, outcome questions per persona, benchmark data) into the flow of work; draft Impact Hypotheses from call notes for AE review; flag deals above threshold missing a metric owner. The agent is how cause 3 ("sellers can't challenge the label") gets fixed at SMB scale — enablement in the deal, not in a classroom.

### 9. Customer Advocacy (existing)
- **Today:** retros (≥100-hour projects), testimonial/case-study/reference pipeline.
- **Customer Impact duties:** time reference asks *after* the first VRR (when there's a result, not just an experience); turn impact records into the quantified case studies that feed role #1's campaigns — closing the loop that makes next year's leads arrive outcome-labeled.

## Coverage map (Envision sub-stage × lead role)

| Sub-stage | Leads | Supporting |
|---|---|---|
| Go-To-Market | Marketing/Demand Gen | Practice Directors (insight content), Sales Ops, Customer Advocacy (proof) |
| Lead Outreach | AE / SDR | Envision Sales Agent |
| Strategic Alignment | AE | Envision Sales Agent, Solution Consultant (on standby) |
| Strategy Session | AE + Solution Consultant | Practice Director (play owner), Envision Sales Agent |
| Solution Meeting | AE + Solution Consultant | Practice Directors (second-practice review already done) |
| EOW Sent | AE | Sales Ops (modular structure, funding), Practice Director (pre-approval) |
| → Evolve flywheel | SAM + Customer Advocacy | Support (20-hour rule), Marketing (case studies) |

## Capability gaps to close (workstreams, not hires)

1. **Insight kits per play** — Practice Directors + Marketing; the pilot's two plays first.
2. **Value-quantification training** — Solution Consultants (+ interested AEs); Khalsa Evidence→Impact method + baseline templates.
3. **Agent content** — load the insight kits and hypothesis drafting into the Envision Sales Agent.
4. **Provenance/mix reporting** — Sales Ops; two new fields at qualification, tags on opportunities.
