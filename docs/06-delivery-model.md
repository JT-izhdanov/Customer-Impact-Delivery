# 06 — Delivery Model

How multi-practice engagements get staffed, run, and measured — entirely within the Proven Process and the three project roles (doc 11).

## Cross-practice pods

- A **pod** is assembled per engagement from the practices named in the play — not negotiated per deal (doc 04, design rule 2).
- A pod is a *staffing mix*, not a role change: it still has exactly one PM, one PL, and Project Developers — the developer pool simply spans the practices the play names.
- Practices retain their benches and utilization accountability; the pod is a staffing pattern, not a re-org.

## Outcome stewardship — within the existing roles (see doc 11)

The project roles are constant: PM, PL, Project Developers. Customer Impact adds **no new role**. Outcome accountability follows existing ownership, stage by stage:

| Stage | Steward | Through existing duties |
|---|---|---|
| Envision | AE/SAM (+ Practice Director) | Outcome discovery, Impact Hypothesis creation, EOW content |
| Plan → Deliver → Launch | **PM** + **PL** | PM: custody of the hypothesis as a governance artifact (baselines, cadence, customer communication — already PM ownership). PL: metric traceability and telemetry (already technical direction) |
| Evolve | Account team (SAM/AE) + Customer Advocacy | Existing SBR/QBR cadence, Trusted Advisor program, retros, case-study pipeline |

Handoffs ride existing mechanisms: the Sales→Delivery kickoff walks the delivery team through the hypothesis; the PM's existing closure duties (transition to support/next phase + retro email to Customer Advocacy) hand the outcome thread to the account team.

Stewardship is the difference between "we finished the project" and "we moved the metric" — and it's assignments of existing duties, not a fourth seat.

## Engagement lifecycle

The impact thread through the Proven Process's existing stages (no gate changes — doc 11; full mapping in doc 02):

| Proven Process stage | Impact artifact | Impact checkpoint (content inside existing gates/meetings) |
|---|---|---|
| Envision (Strategy Session → EOW) | Value Baseline + Impact Hypothesis | Customer exec signs the hypothesis; it rides inside the Flag Plant |
| Plan (Kickoff → Discovery → Document) | Measurement plan (who, what data, cadence) | Baseline data actually captured — the substance of "Customer Impact confirmed" |
| Deliver | Metric-tagged stories, telemetry tasks | Mid-phase impact check in existing retros ("still building what moves the metric?") |
| Launch | Adoption plan activated, T₀ recorded | Usage telemetry flowing before users arrive |
| Evolve (Validate/Manage) | Quarterly **Value Realization Review** via the SBR cadence | Metric vs. baseline vs. target, documented as an impact record |

## Value Realization Reviews (VRR)

- **Not a new meeting.** The VRR is the impact agenda delivered through the Proven Process's existing Evolve machinery: the Validate sub-stage and the SBR/QBR cadence the account teams already run (~70/quarter). Concretely: the SBR's existing first 15 minutes (adoption/usage validation) present the Impact Scorecard — metric vs. baseline vs. target.
- Quarterly, with the executive who signed the Impact Hypothesis; first one 60–90 days post-go-live.
- Standing agenda: metric movement · adoption data · blockers · **next-highest-impact opportunity** (the structured cross-sell moment, fed back to account planning).
- Output: a one-page impact record. These accumulate into case-study raw material (Customer Advocacy's existing pipeline) and the evidence base for doc 08 metrics.
- First VRR is scheduled during phase kickoff, not after go-live — it's in the EOW.

## Measurement infrastructure

- Adoption telemetry from the Microsoft stack (M365 Copilot dashboards, D365 usage, Power BI consumption, Secure Score) is our friend — standardize per-play dashboards so VRRs are cheap to run.
- Business metrics come from the customer; the measurement plan names the customer data owner. When customers can't produce the number, that's a billable D&AI workstream (and often the wedge into the account).

## Managed services tie-in

- Post-go-live impact needs someone tending it. Attach a light managed/optimization retainer to each play so VRRs, adoption nudges, and small enhancements have a commercial home.
- This converts project revenue into recurring revenue and keeps us in the account between phases — the retention mechanism for the whole model.

## What does NOT change

- **The Proven Process and the three project roles — constants, per doc 11.** Everything above is content flowing through existing stages, gates, meetings, and roles.
- Practice-level delivery methodologies, QA, and tooling stay as they are.
- Utilization stays a practice metric (attribution handles the cross-charging — doc 07).
- Small single-practice projects run exactly as today.
