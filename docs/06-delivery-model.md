# 06 — Delivery Model

How multi-practice engagements get staffed, run, and measured.

## Cross-practice pods

- A **pod** is assembled per engagement from the practices named in the play — not negotiated per deal (doc 04, design rule 2).
- Typical shape: lead-practice engagement manager + consultants from each contributing practice, sized to the phase.
- Practices retain their benches and utilization accountability; the pod is an overlay, not a re-org.

## The Outcome Owner role

Every Customer Impact engagement has a named **Outcome Owner**:

- Owns the Impact Hypothesis end-to-end: baseline → delivery alignment → VRR.
- Is the customer executive's single point of contact for "is this working?"
- Runs the Value Realization Reviews and produces the impact record.
- Typically a senior consultant or engagement manager from the lead practice; this is a **hat, not a hire** in 2027. If the motion scales, it may become a dedicated role in 2028.

The Outcome Owner is the difference between "we finished the project" and "we moved the metric."

## Engagement lifecycle

| Phase | Standard artifact | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery (entry-point assessment) | Value Baseline + Impact Hypothesis | Customer exec signs the hypothesis |
| Phase kickoff | Measurement plan (who, what data, cadence) | Baseline data actually captured |
| Delivery | Normal practice-level delivery artifacts | Deliverable gates **plus** a mid-phase impact check ("are we still building the thing that moves the metric?") |
| Go-live | Adoption plan activated | Usage telemetry flowing |
| Post go-live | Quarterly **Value Realization Review** | Metric vs. baseline vs. target, documented |

## Value Realization Reviews (VRR)

- Quarterly, with the executive who signed the Impact Hypothesis.
- Standing agenda: metric movement · adoption data · blockers · **next-highest-impact opportunity** (the structured cross-sell moment, fed back to account planning).
- Output: a one-page impact record. These accumulate into case-study raw material and the evidence base for doc 08 metrics.
- First VRR is scheduled during phase kickoff, not after go-live — it's in the SOW.

## Measurement infrastructure

- Adoption telemetry from the Microsoft stack (M365 Copilot dashboards, D365 usage, Power BI consumption, Secure Score) is our friend — standardize per-play dashboards so VRRs are cheap to run.
- Business metrics come from the customer; the measurement plan names the customer data owner. When customers can't produce the number, that's a billable D&AI workstream (and often the wedge into the account).

## Managed services tie-in

- Post-go-live impact needs someone tending it. Attach a light managed/optimization retainer to each play so VRRs, adoption nudges, and small enhancements have a commercial home.
- This converts project revenue into recurring revenue and keeps us in the account between phases — the retention mechanism for the whole model.

## What does NOT change

- Practice-level delivery methodologies, QA, and tooling stay as they are.
- Utilization stays a practice metric (attribution handles the cross-charging — doc 07).
- Small single-practice projects run exactly as today.
