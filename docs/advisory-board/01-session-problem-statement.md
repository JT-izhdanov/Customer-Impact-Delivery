Unanimous: a genuinely mechanism-level diagnosis (Rumelt: "a real diagnosis, not a list of symptoms — a rarity"), but incomplete in four converging ways.

## Key arguments

1. **The trust gap is missing** (Maister, Rumelt, Block — all citing Gardner in research 02): merged firms specifically lack *competence trust*; all six original causes were mechanical. Fix crediting and a seller still won't introduce a colleague they don't trust to their client.
2. **A capability gap is misdiagnosed as a motivation gap** (Dixon/Adamson): sellers *can't* challenge tech labels — no commercial-insight content exists to challenge with — not just *won't*. Also missing: the commoditization cost of accepting customer self-diagnosis (price-shopped, line-by-line comparable).
3. **The economic diagnosis was organizational-only** (Lah): the deeper failure is a Land-only revenue model in a LAER economy, plus the secular threat (AI compressing effort-based delivery) that makes 2027 urgent *regardless* of the merger. Undeclared transition costs ("the fish") get killed by the CFO in Q2.
4. **No crux, no choices** (Rumelt, Lafley/Martin): six flat causes invite six equal workstreams ("proto-dog's-dinner"); the lesson table implies where-to-play choices it never makes; nothing is excluded or sequenced.
5. **Leadership's 50% was absent; the diagnosed weren't in the room** (Block): all causes located in sellers/systems/Microsoft, none in the leadership that kept silo comp while announcing collaboration. A diagnosis *at* people invites compliance-nodding and quiet non-adoption — the merger's own failure mode.

## Tensions surfaced

- **Rumelt vs. the seats adding causes:** Rumelt demands simplification toward a crux while Maister/Lah/Dixon-Adamson each added a cause. Resolution applied: causes added (completeness), plus a proposed-crux section (concentration) — the crux *choice* escalated to Igor.
- **Block vs. the document's genre:** a leadership diagnosis vs. a contracting artifact co-owned by the diagnosed. Escalated to Igor (socialization decision) rather than resolved.
