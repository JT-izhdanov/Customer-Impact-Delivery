# 11 — What Is NOT Changing

*Declared by Igor Zhdanov, Aug 2026. These are constants of the 2027 Customer Impact plan — design constraints, not preferences. Every other document in this repo conforms to them.*

## The constants

1. **The Proven Process.** All five stages (Envision → Plan → Deliver → Launch → Evolve), their sub-stages, exit gates, and metrics remain exactly as documented in `docs/proven-process/`. Customer Impact adds **no new stages, no new gates, no new required meetings.**
2. **The three project roles.** Every project keeps exactly three roles — **Project Manager** (delivery governance), **Project Lead** (technical direction), **Project Developers** (pool of technical resources) — governed by the PM/PL Partnership operating standard. Customer Impact adds **no new roles and no amendments to the standard.**
3. **The four-practice structure.** Data & AI · Copilot & Dynamics · Security & Compliance (incl. Azure) · Business Central ERP. No reorg (per the session-01 diagnosis: structure was never the problem).
4. **Practice-level delivery methodologies, QA, and tooling.**
5. **Small single-stack work** below the deal threshold runs exactly as today.

## The design rule that follows

> **Customer Impact adds content that flows through existing structures — never new structure.**

Allowed: new artifacts (Impact Hypothesis, Value Baseline, Impact Scorecard) carried inside existing artifacts and repositories; new agenda content inside existing meetings (SBR first-15-minutes, kickoff walkthrough, retro question); new columns/tags on existing artifacts (story-to-metric traceability); new interpretation of existing undefined terms (what "Customer Impact confirmed" means at the Plan gate); new sales content (plays, insight narratives, sequences).

Not allowed: new exit-gate items; new stages or sub-stages; new project roles or role-standard amendments; parallel lifecycles, parallel review meetings, or shadow processes.

## How outcome accountability works within the constants

The plan originally sketched an "Outcome Owner" role. With the roles constant, outcome accountability instead **follows existing ownership, stage by stage** — which the Proven Process supports naturally because its stages already assign the right owners:

| Stage | Outcome accountability lives with | Through their existing duties |
|---|---|---|
| Envision | AE/SAM (+ Practice Director pre-approval) | Outcome discovery, Impact Hypothesis creation, EOW content |
| Plan → Deliver → Launch | **PM** (custody of the hypothesis as a governance artifact: baselines, cadence, communication) + **PL** (metric traceability, telemetry, technical feasibility of measurement) | Exactly the ownership split the PM/PL standard already defines — the hypothesis is one more governed artifact, the telemetry one more technical deliverable |
| Evolve | **Account team (SAM/AE) + Customer Advocacy** | The existing SBR/QBR cadence (70/quarter), Trusted Advisor program, retros, and case-study pipeline |

The handoffs ride existing mechanisms: Sales→Delivery kickoff (hypothesis walkthrough), and the PM's existing closure duty ("transition to support or next-phase delivery" + retro email to Customer Advocacy) hands the outcome thread to the account team. No RASIC exemptions, no persistent project roles after close.

## Why stating this matters

- **Strategically** (Rumelt, session 01): a visible not-changing list is what makes the changing list a choice rather than a wish.
- **Emotionally** (Block, session 01): the people asked to change behavior need to see that the ground they stand on — process, roles, structure — is stable. This is evolution on a proven base, not another upheaval.
- **Practically:** the Proven Process already promises outcomes at every stage (Envision's "Business Outcomes," Plan's "Customer Impact confirmed" gate, Launch's "outcomes realized?" question, Evolve's "validate impact"). **The 2027 plan is the instrumentation of promises the Proven Process already makes** — numbers, owners, and dates for language that already exists.
